the length-limited fields; everything here is written to be accurate to the
app as it ships today — fully offline, no accounts, no ads, nothing collected.

> feed, and name. No ads, no accounts, no internet needed. Just crayons and wonder.

### Keywords  (≤ 100 chars, comma-separated, no spaces)
`draw,kids,toddler,preschool,ABC,letters,numbers,shapes,tracing,learn,creatures,offline,no ads,pen`

### Description  (≤ 4000 chars)

Drawlings turns a child's drawing into a friend.

Draw a fish, and it swims off to live in a coral reef. Draw a rocket, and it
blasts around a galaxy. Whatever your child scribbles comes alive in its own
little world — one they can visit any time to say hello, feed a treat, and watch
their creations wiggle, blink, and play.

It's the feeling every kid wishes were real: *I drew it, and it came alive.*

WHAT'S INSIDE

• Draw anything — wobbly lines make the best creatures. Nothing is ever "wrong."
• Watch it hatch and come alive, then set it free into a living world.
• Five worlds to fill: Magic Reef, Giggle Galaxy, Sunny Farm, Dino Island, and
  My World — a world your child paints themselves.
• Care for your creatures — feed them a treat you draw, give them a name, make
  one your pet. They're still there tomorrow.
• Learn while you play: trace letters, numbers, shapes, and whole words. Each
  one comes alive when it's done.
• A sticker book that keeps every drawing your child has ever made.
• One warm recorded voice reads every letter, number, and word aloud.

GENTLE BY DESIGN

Nothing is timed, nothing is scored against other kids, and nothing is ever
marked wrong — the stars only ever say "so close, try again," and every try
counts. There are no streaks to keep up and no creature is ever taken away.

MADE FOR PARENTS TO TRUST

• No ads. Not one, ever.
• No accounts and no sign-in.
• Nothing about your child is collected — there is no analytics and no tracking.
• Works with NO internet at all. On a plane, in a waiting room, anywhere.
• Everything your child makes is saved on your device and never leaves it.
• Sharing or printing a drawing sits behind a simple grown-up question, so a
  child can't send anything off the device on their own.

Built to comply with COPPA and made for children first.

Drawlings is a quiet, ad-free corner of the tablet where a four-year-old's
imagination is the whole point — and where the crayon always wins.

### What's New  (first release)
> Hello! This is Drawlings's first version. Draw something, and watch it live.
