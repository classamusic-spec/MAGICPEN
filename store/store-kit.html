Magic Pen turns a child's drawing into a friend. Draw a fish, and it swims off to live in a coral reef. Draw a rocket, and it blasts around a galaxy. Whatever your child scribbles comes alive in its own little world — one they can visit any time to say hello, feed a treat, and watch their creations wiggle, blink, and play. It's the feeling every kid wishes were real: I drew it, and it came alive. WHAT'S INSIDE • Draw anything — wobbly lines make the best creatures. Nothing is ever "wrong." • Watch it hatch and come alive, then set it free into a living world. • Five worlds to fill: Magic Reef, Giggle Galaxy, Sunny Farm, Dino Island, and My World — a world your child paints themselves. • Care for your creatures — feed them a treat you draw, give them a name, make one your pet. GENTLE BY DESIGN Nothing is timed, nothing is scored against other kids, and nothing is ever marked wrong — the stars only ever say "so close, try again," and every try counts. There are no streaks to keep up and no creature is ever taken away. MADE FOR PARENTS TO TRUST • No ads. On a plane, in a waiting room, anywhere. • Everything your child makes is saved on your device and never leaves it. • Sharing or printing a drawing sits behind a simple grown-up question. Privacy & data safety

the whole answer, both consoles
✓
No data collected
No server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking — and no network requests at all. Everything a child makes stays in on-device storage.

Apple — App Privacy

  • Data collection“No, we do not collect data from this app.” Removes every follow-up.
  • EncryptionITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false (already in Info.plist).

Google Play — Data safety

  • Collects or shares data?No — no data types.
  • Delete my data?Yes — clearing storage or uninstalling erases everything; no server copy.
PermissionPlatformWhy
NoneiOSNo device permissions are declared, so the app can prompt for none.
NoneAndroidOnly INTERNET is present (Capacitor default, unused); no runtime permission is requested.

The user-facing policy ships at public/privacy.html — host it and paste its URL into both consoles.

Getting it shipped

  1. Confirm the bundle id com.classamusic.magicpen before creating the app records — it is frozen after first submission.
  2. Host public/privacy.html and put its URL in both consoles.
  3. Build & upload: npm run build && npx cap sync, then Xcode → Archive (iOS) and bundleRelease signed (Android).
  4. Fill the listing text from this kit and upload the screenshots.
  5. Answer both privacy questionnaires: no data collected.
  6. Complete the kids programs — App Store Kids category, Google Play Families.

Still needed by the consoles, not generated here: an App Store preview video (optional) and a Play 1024 × 500 feature graphic.
