Privacy

Magic Pen · last updated 25 August 2026

Magic Pen collects nothing about your child. There is no account, no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics. Everything a child draws or practises is kept on your own device.

Magic Pen is made for young children, so this page is written to be read in a minute and to say exactly what happens — no legal fog.

1 What stays on the device

When a child draws a creature, traces a letter, or earns a star, that is saved in your browser's own storage on this device. It never leaves it. That includes:

  • the crayon drawings and the creatures made from them,
  • which letters, numbers, words and shapes have been traced,
  • the stars earned and the "days drawing" count,
  • a few settings, such as whether sound is on.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Clearing your browser's data, or removing the app, erases all of it. There is no copy anywhere for us to hold, because we never received one.

2 What we never collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store any of the following:

  • names, email addresses, or phone numbers of a child;
  • birthdays, ages, photos of a face, or a voice recording;
  • location, contacts, or device identifiers used for tracking;
  • any account or login of any kind.

There are no third-party trackers, no advertising networks, and no social-media buttons in Magic Pen. The fonts and every picture are packaged inside the app, so simply opening it makes no request to any outside company.

3 The one thing that can leave the device

Magic Pen has one optional feature called “magic dust.” It turns a child's crayon drawing into a more polished picture. To do that, that one drawing is sent over the internet to an art service, which sends a picture back.

This is the only thing in Magic Pen that ever leaves the device, and:

  • it is switched off until a grown-up turns it on;
  • turning it on requires answering a simple grown-up question first, so small hands can't switch it on by themselves;
  • only the drawing is sent — no name, no account, nothing else — and the art service is asked not to keep it;
  • the child's original crayon drawing is always kept, exactly as drawn;
  • it can be switched off again at any time under For grown-ups → Magic dust, and from then on nothing is sent.

If you never turn magic dust on, Magic Pen makes no internet requests at all while your child uses it.

4 Made for children (COPPA)

Magic Pen is designed for children under 13 and is built to comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar rules elsewhere. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing to sell, share, or hand to a data broker — and there never will be.

The one online feature, magic dust, is placed behind a parental step by design, so a child cannot send anything off the device without a grown-up.

5 Your controls

  • See what's been practised: the For grown-ups screen shows progress, read from this device.
  • Turn magic dust on or off: also under For grown-ups, behind the grown-up question.
  • Erase everything: clear this app's data in your browser or device settings, or remove the app. That deletes all drawings and progress permanently.

6 Changes & contact

If this policy ever changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top and the notice inside the app. We won't quietly start collecting things this page says we don't.

Questions from a parent or guardian are welcome at classamusic@gmail.com.

Magic Pen is a drawing toy for young children. No ads. No accounts. No data collected.
